# Refund policy

Paste into **Settings → Policies → Refund policy**. Replaces the current 14-day, no-exchange version.

Two substantive changes from what's live now:

1. **30 days from delivery**, not 14. Standard shipping is 7–15 business days, so a 14-day window
   punished customers for your fulfilment speed.
2. **Exchanges are allowed.** The current policy says "We do not offer direct exchanges." For a
   children's apparel store sized in centimetres off a supplier chart, wrong-size is the number one
   return reason and an exchange is what the parent actually wants. Refusing exchanges converts a
   retained sale into a refund plus a lost customer.

## RETURN & EXCHANGE POLICY

Last updated: August 2026

We want your child's costume to fit and to arrive in time for the big day. If something isn't
right, we'll make it right.

### Returns

We accept return and exchange requests within **30 days of delivery**. To be eligible, items must be
unworn, unwashed, and in original condition with tags attached.

Costumes that have been worn for a performance are not returnable, so please try the costume on as
soon as it arrives rather than waiting for the day of the event.

### Exchanges — including free size swaps

If the size isn't right, we will exchange it. Email us within 30 days of delivery and we'll send the
correct size.

**On your first size exchange for any order, we cover return shipping.** Children's sizing is hard
to get right from a chart, and we would rather you get the right fit than eat the cost of our size
guide.

### How to request a return or exchange

Email **phil@ivorycrowncollective.com** with:

- your order number
- what you'd like (refund, or exchange plus the size you need)
- a photo, if the item arrived damaged or incorrect

We respond within 1 business day with a prepaid label or next steps.

### Refunds

Once your return is received and inspected, we'll email you to confirm approval. Approved refunds go
back to your original payment method within 5–7 business days. Depending on your bank, it can take
another 2–5 days to appear on your statement.

Original shipping charges are refunded only if the item was damaged, incorrect, or arrived after a
guaranteed date we gave you in writing.

### Non-returnable items

- Costumes that have been worn or performed in
- Wigs, tights, and hosiery, once the sealed packaging is opened (hygiene)
- Face paint, makeup, and adhesive prosthetics
- Final sale and clearance items, clearly marked as such at checkout

### Damaged, incorrect, or missing items

Contact us within **7 days of delivery** with a photo and we will replace it at no cost to you, or
refund you in full — your choice. You do not need to ship the damaged item back.

We extended this from the previous 48-hour window because packages often sit unopened until the
week of an event.

### Late arrivals for a dated event

If you told us your event date at checkout and the costume arrives after it, contact us and we will
refund you in full including shipping, whether or not you keep the item. Our
[Costume by Date guide](/pages/costume-by-date) shows how far ahead to order.

### Order cancellations

Orders can be cancelled for a full refund any time before they ship — usually a 1–3 business day
window. Email us as soon as possible and we'll try to catch it.
