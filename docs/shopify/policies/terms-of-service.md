### 5. Safety and intended use — please read

Our costumes are **novelty and theatrical garments intended for supervised dress-up, stage
performance, and costume events.** They are not protective equipment, sleepwear, or daily wear.

- **Flame resistance.** Costumes are not flame-resistant unless a product page explicitly says so.
  Keep children away from open flames, candles, jack-o'-lanterns, stage pyrotechnics, and heat
  sources while in costume.
- **Adult supervision required.** Children must be supervised while wearing costumes, particularly
  any item with a mask, hood, cape, train, or trailing fabric, which can obstruct vision or create
  a trip hazard.
- **Small parts.** Accessories such as buttons, beads, glasses, jewellery, buckles, and props may
  present a choking hazard. Not suitable for children under 3 unless a product page states
  otherwise.
- **Capes and cords.** Items with neck ties or cords carry a strangulation risk. Do not leave a
  child unattended in them, and never use them for sleep or in a crib or play structure.
- **Visibility.** For outdoor evening use such as trick-or-treating, add reflective tape or carry a
  light. Dark costumes are hard for drivers to see.
- **Not sleepwear.** These garments do not meet children's sleepwear flammability standards. Do not
  use them as pyjamas.

By purchasing, you accept responsibility for supervising use and for judging whether an item is
appropriate for your child's age and abilities.
