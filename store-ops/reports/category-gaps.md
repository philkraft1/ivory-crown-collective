# Category gap research — kids & costume retail

**Scope:** Product *types* / assortment gaps only. No competitor titles, photos, descriptions, or scraped listings.

**References observed (category menus / public positioning, Aug 2026 pass):** Party City, Spirit Halloween, and common kids-costume Shopify storefront patterns (age bands, licensed characters, book day, accessories).

**Store under review:** Ivory Crown costume store (`1wtpc0-c2`) — assortment should favor school book-day, kids dress-up, and stage — not adult party novelty.

---

## Priority 1 — Stock first (high demand, fits kids/school)

| # | Product type to stock | Why it gaps / wins | Suggested tags |
|---|----------------------|--------------------|----------------|
| 1 | Licensed-style **princess / royal** costumes (sizes 4–6X and 7–8) | Core kids demand year-round + Halloween | `age:little-kids`, `occasion:halloween`, `occasion:everyday`, `type:costume` |
| 2 | **Book Character Day** packs: teacher-friendly STEM / literacy characters (scientist, astronaut, historical author *archetypes* — no copying a specific retailer’s exclusive IP kit) | School calendar drives bursts; many general costume shops under-serve classroom-safe looks | `age:little-kids`, `age:big-kids`, `occasion:book-character`, `type:costume` |
| 3 | **Animal / nature** onesies & jumpsuits (toddler + little kids) | Easy fit, high giftability, everyday dress-up | `age:toddler`, `age:little-kids`, `occasion:everyday`, `type:costume` |
| 4 | **Accessory packs** (wand + crown, mask + cape, glasses + prop book) sold standalone | Margin + basket add-on; competitors bury these | `type:accessory`, matching occasion tags |
| 5 | **Superhero / hero cape sets** (generic, non-infringing silhouettes) | Universal boys/girls demand | `age:little-kids`, `occasion:halloween`, `occasion:everyday`, `type:costume` |

## Priority 2 — Strong seasonal / event

| # | Product type | Notes | Tags |
|---|--------------|-------|------|
| 6 | Halloween **group / sibling** coordinating sets | Underserved vs single SKUs | `occasion:halloween`, multi age tags |
| 7 | **Stage / recital** basics: black jazz pants, leotard-friendly overlays, simple character robes | Party City skews novelty; dance parents need simpler | `occasion:stage`, `type:costume` / `type:accessory` |

| 10 | Career / community helpers (doctor, firefighter, chef) for classroom | Book-day adjacent | `occasion:book-character`, `occasion:everyday` |

---

## Explicitly out of scope / avoid

- Adult risqué / scary gore as primary assortment (wrong brand fit)
- Copying another Shopify store’s photos, titles, or descriptions
- Unlicensed use of major entertainment trademarks — buy from authorized distributors or use generic archetypes
